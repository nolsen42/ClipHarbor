One more step: get the ClipHarbor app

The extension only sends links. The free ClipHarbor app does the downloading, so it needs to be on this computer too.

Download ClipHarbor
Other systems
  1. Install the app you just downloaded.
  2. Open it once. That lets your browser send it links.
  3. Right-click a video and choose “Download with ClipHarbor”. When your browser asks whether to open ClipHarbor, tick “Always allow” and press Open.

Already have the app? Then you're all set: just open it once if you haven't yet.
